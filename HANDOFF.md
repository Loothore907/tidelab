## Decisions carried forward

TideLab is an evidence-controlled, multi-venue and multi-asset-class strategy research platform. The owner's long-horizon goal is consistent, profitable operation after realistic costs and risk, not rapid gains or a near-term income target. The platform should keep finding and registering candidate strategies, testing them honestly, observing frozen candidates on newly arriving data in paper mode, and retiring weakened strategies while researching replacements. Only sustained evidence and a separate owner decision could lead to a small real-money pilot. Engineering and learning are valuable outcomes but are not substitutes for trading evidence.

Coinbase Advanced Trade BTC-USD remains the first public-data fixture; OKX BTC-USDT is the selected local historical fixture; and the initial hourly trend hypothesis remains the first baseline experiment. None defines the permanent scope or a preferred investment. The owner is not being asked to choose promising pairs: TideLab's research process should define an affordable eligible market universe, screen for candidates, test them against registered strategies, and retain every rejected candidate and trial. Breadth-first screening needs comparable permitted history; shortlisted markets can justify deeper data only when a measured question requires it. Universe, ranking rules, costs, trial budget, and untouched evaluation periods must be fixed before looking at outcomes. This candidate-discovery process is a direction, not an implemented scanner or a validated edge. The core will use canonical research contracts, capability-aware venue/product adapters, versioned strategies, deterministic replay, a complete experiment registry, independent risk decisions, and reconciled accounting.

TideLab-authored work is Apache-2.0 and is intended for eventual public release. Pinned LEAN is selected as the first synthetic historical/local-paper integration target. TideLab owns replaceable strategy/risk policy, conservative costs, data, paper authority, and research evidence; use LEAN's public engine interfaces and avoid an upstream fork. NautilusTrader's tested backed external-data recovery gap remains recorded as an alternative limitation. Distribution and dependency obligations remain later gates.

Coinbase's current market-data terms create a material research-use and public-reproducibility concern in addition to the observed 349-hour depth. Pending TL-001B, do not fetch new Coinbase data for strategy development or automated analysis, and keep existing data and derived artifacts local. The owner's 2026-09-22 priority is a sufficiently deep dataset for local scripted research and forward-paper testing, with supportable research, retention, and automated-analysis rights. Public raw datasets and real-data-derived charts/reports/video are optional and assessed separately before publication.
