# TL-001B Bitstamp rights correction

On 2026-09-24, PR #57 selected Bitstamp's public BTC/USD hourly OHLCV endpoint for local research and briefly closed issue #3. That conclusion was too strong. [Bitstamp's public API documentation](https://www.bitstamp.net/api/) documents technical access and directs companies seeking commercial data use to a separate license. It does not expressly grant private retention or scripted strategy analysis. A public endpoint plus a commercial-license requirement does not establish those rights. The repository's unknown-rights gate therefore still applies.

The technical probe fetched 40,896 contiguous hourly rows from 2022-01-01 through 2026-09-01 UTC and a 528-hour recent bridge, with basic timestamp/OHLCV checks. Those observations establish plausible depth and forward continuity only. All six locally generated CSV/manifest files were removed on 2026-09-24 without strategy evaluation or publication. The fetcher and selection record were reverted. Bitstamp remains a technical candidate, not a selected research source.

The [CryptoDataDownload archive](https://www.cryptodatadownload.com/data/) advertises a free noncommercial license and deep data, but its [terms](https://www.cryptodatadownload.com/terms-of-use/) expressly disclaim verification and possible third-party intellectual-property restrictions. [Gemini's Market Data Agreement](https://www.gemini.com/legal/market-data-agreement) expressly permits personal/internal download, storage and price analysis but binds use to a linked fee schedule that was unavailable/404 during this check; exact candle depth is also unverified. [CoinAPI](https://www.coinapi.io/usage-policy) describes quantitative research as ordinary internal use, but its [binding agreement](https://www.coinapi.io/legal) leaves external-source licensing to the customer. Its [pricing](https://www.coinapi.io/products/market-data-api/pricing) starts with metered $25 credits or a $79/month Startup plan. Paying for it would not, by itself, resolve underlying source rights. No account, subscription, or provider contact was made.

**Disposition:** Issue #3 is reopened. Kraken is no longer a schedule dependency. No real-data strategy evaluation may start until a source has a documented local retention and automated-analysis basis, exact-pair depth, and a forward bridge. Continue TL-002 synthetic runtime controls under issue #48 and prepare a frozen TL-003 strategy protocol without opening real-data results. A later source decision may choose a permitted free source or a specific paid license after its terms and total cost are established; no spend or new provider outreach is authorized by this record. Public real-data artifacts remain a separate rights question.
