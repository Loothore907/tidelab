# TL-002: synthetic correction after H1 handoff

Date: 2026-09-25. Owner: [issue #48](https://github.com/Loothore907/tidelab/issues/48). All prices, orders, balances and snapshots here are invented. No external broker or market data was accessed.

An H1 successor created by terminal handoff now has a source-specific claim guard in SQLite. The ordinary paper-intent claim refuses it. Before the pinned LEAN mock routes the successor order, the guarded claim compares a fresh complete parent report and two matching account/order/execution snapshots with the hashes accepted at handoff. The comparison and transition to `submission_unknown` share a SQLite write transaction. A changed parent report or stable but changed snapshot latches a durable authority hold with a reason and evidence hash; later claims and preparations refuse the database even after restart or restoration of the original fixture. There is no automatic hold clearing or corrected-execution rewrite.

The focused pinned LEAN probe copied the post-handoff database, changed the terminal report's execution price, cash and revision, and attempted the successor. The claim held before LEAN order routing: `gate=claim_holds new_submissions=0`; no successor report was written. A retry with the original report remained blocked by the persisted hold. The unchanged branch of the synthetic probe still routed its successor once. Focused Python tests also cover a changed snapshot observed after a successor was claimed and verify that future paper authority remains held. The pinned build passed with zero errors.

This is a local-file, single-writer safety check. It relies on the caller supplying current complete reads; a correction after those reads can still race with a new order. A real adapter must show how its source can give consistent, repeatable account/order/execution evidence, how changes are observed, and how corrected executions are reversed or replaced before any hold can be cleared. This slice does not establish external finality, unattended paper safety, engine deployment, or live authority. Issue [#3](https://github.com/Loothore907/tidelab/issues/3) separately owns timely forward data and rights.
