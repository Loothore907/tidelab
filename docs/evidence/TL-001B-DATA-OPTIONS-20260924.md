## Decision and trigger

1. Continue TL-002's synthetic LEAN, policy, ledger and recovery work under [issue #48](https://github.com/Loothore907/tidelab/issues/48). Do not hold that engineering work for a provider reply.
2. Keep TL-001B open and Kraken off the critical path. Check the existing Gemini correspondence once at the next pickup. If it provides the binding fee, research/retention scope, exact-pair hourly depth, and forward continuity, verify those terms before a bounded technical sample. If it does not, define one small objective candidate universe and compare one rights-documented no-spend source for cross-market breadth and timely recording; report the measured gap before any capped paid proposal. The owner does not need to choose a pair. A source is selected only after rights, technical coverage, and effective cost are each established.
3. For the first historical test, obtain enough consecutive hourly bars to span distinct market conditions, verify UTC boundaries/gaps/revisions and forward continuity, and use conservative cost/fill assumptions. A bar archive cannot validate bid/ask spread, order queue or intrabar execution. Add quote/trade data only when that precision is needed.
4. Keep data spend at $0 while the research foundation and candidate screening remain unproven. If a free source leaves a specific material gap, return with one explicit capped paid proposal. Include actual price and overage cap, billing commitment, dataset scope, research/retention rights, source-specific restrictions, and cancellation consequences before spending. Public charts/reports/video remain a separate optional gate.

This screen selected no timely or multi-market source, and created no strategy profitability evidence. The later OKX historical selection is recorded above.
