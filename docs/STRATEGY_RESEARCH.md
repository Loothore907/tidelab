# Strategy research protocol

This protocol prevents TideLab from confusing a profitable-looking historical simulation with an investable edge. It applies to public strategies, academic research, open-source implementations, and TideLab strategies generated by a person or model.

## Research objective

Find strategies whose rules and feasible execution environment show positive expectancy after realistic costs, acceptable drawdown, and useful benchmark comparison. Historical results nominate candidates. Immutable forward-paper results test whether the candidate and operating system behave on newly arriving data. Income feasibility is assessed only after an edge survives those stages.

The platform may support multiple venues and product types. Each experiment remains narrow, versioned, reproducible, and bound to explicit data and execution assumptions.

## Strategy manifest

Register a manifest before evaluation containing:

- strategy identifier, version, author/source, and economic rationale;
- supported instrument/product class and required venue capabilities;
- required data, interval, feature definitions, warmup, and staleness limits;
- exact entry, exit, target exposure, sizing, and existing-position rules;
- parameters, permitted sensitivity range, and maximum variant/search budget;
- signal timing and earliest permissible execution time;
- execution mode and cost/fill assumptions;
- risk constraints and fail-closed conditions;
- development, validation, and untouched evaluation dates;
- benchmarks, metrics, and numeric pass/fail criteria;
- source revision, data revision, configuration hash, and dependencies.
- data-license/terms revision and whether retention, redistribution, derived charts/reports, video demonstration, and automated analysis are permitted, prohibited, or unknown.

Changing any rule, parameter outside its registered range, data boundary, execution assumption, or pass criterion creates a new experiment version.

## Experiment record

Keep an append-only record of every run, including failures and discarded variants. Record inputs, outputs, warnings, data gaps, code/config identity, environment, timestamps, and the relationship to prior runs. A model-generated variation counts as a trial even if it is never highlighted in a report.

Research reproducibility does not override provider rights. Restricted market data and derived artifacts remain local and out of Git. Public fixtures must be synthetic or expressly redistributable, and public results must identify how an authorized user can reacquire or verify the permitted inputs. Unknown publication rights fail closed.

Do not use the untouched interval as an iterative development set. Once inspected, it belongs to that experiment generation. Further adaptation requires a newly declared untouched period or honest forward data.

## Historical evaluation

1. Validate timestamps, closure/completeness, ordering, duplicates, gaps, product rules, and provenance.
2. Test the implementation for future-data leakage, same-bar execution, impossible fills, accounting errors, and nondeterminism.
3. Develop only within the declared development period and search budget.
4. Evaluate chronologically, using walk-forward validation where appropriate and one untouched final period.
5. Execute after the signal at the next feasible opportunity. Model fees, spread, slippage, latency, minimums, rounding, partial/missed fills, and venue-specific failure modes.
6. Compare with cash and relevant passive benchmarks over identical dates and starting capital, including benchmark costs and exposure.
7. Report net expectancy, drawdown, time underwater, turnover, exposure, trade count, uncertainty, regime behavior, parameter sensitivity, and dependence on the largest wins.
8. Stress worse execution and costs. Reject an edge that exists only under perfect or unverified assumptions.
9. Account for the number and dependence of trials when interpreting the selected winner. Add search-aware measures such as deflated performance or probability-of-overfitting analysis when the experiment count and sample support them.

## Forward evaluation

- Freeze strategy, parameters, risk rules, code/config identity, and evaluation criteria before the run.
- Operate on newly arriving data in simulated mode first. Record outages, stale data, missed decisions, recovery, and modeled execution discrepancies.
- Any logic change creates a new version and restarts its evidence window. Operational repairs that claim no strategy change require deterministic before/after replay evidence.
- The initial review floor remains at least eight weeks and 50 closed trades, extending as needed. It is neither statistical proof nor an automatic live gate.
- Advisory mode, if separately approved, creates a time-limited proposal and records the user's actual execution or rejection. Never treat a proposal as a fill.

## Initial hypothesis family

The first research cycle may implement a deliberately small baseline set after TL-001 and TL-002:

- cash and buy-and-hold benchmarks;
- a simple moving-average trend filter;
- time-series momentum;
- a channel/breakout rule;
- volatility-scaled trend;
- one simple mean-reversion rule as a contrasting hypothesis.

These are research baselines, not recommendations or claimed edges. Market making, sniping, leverage, derivatives, multi-asset rotation, and machine learning require separate hypotheses and gates.

## Venue and product extension

A strategy declares capabilities rather than naming a venue where practical. The adapter advertises what it supports, and the match fails closed when requirements are absent or ambiguous.

Canonical contracts cover shared economic facts; native extensions preserve material differences:

- centralized spot: balances, order books, orders, fills, custody, and venue fees;
- event contracts: event definition, expiry, outcome source, settlement, payout, and market-specific restrictions;
- on-chain markets: quote/route identity, wallet authority, transaction construction, simulation, network and priority fees, expiration, confirmation, program state, and MEV exposure;
- future securities products: sessions, corporate actions, settlement, borrowing/shorting, and product-specific regulation.

The second adapter is a portability test, not an excuse to implement every venue. No adapter advances beyond public/read-only or paper operation without its own current eligibility, legal/product, API-terms, cost, security, recovery, and authority review.

## Income feasibility

Positive expectancy, return regularity, and income capacity are separate findings. Only after forward evidence exists, estimate:

- plausible net return distribution and drawdown duration;
- capital required for the desired dollar outcome;
- liquidity/capacity and how costs change with size;
- turnover, tax/reporting burden, infrastructure, and monitoring costs;
- concentration and dependence on rare large wins;
- whether withdrawals would impair compounding or risk limits.

Do not increase trade count, leverage, or risk merely to manufacture monthly income. If the economics do not support the objective, close the experiment honestly.
